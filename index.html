03

What's working, what's not

✅ Working

  • Reels are the engine. 57.3% of all interactions and the entire top-content list. This is where to put your effort.
  • The hook style wins. White text plus one cyan keyword (A DRONE, BEHIND THE SCENES, CAMERA POV). Your four best reels all use it.
  • Reaching new people. 66% of views and 18.6% of interactions came from non-followers. Reels are pushing you past your existing base.
  • Shares beat comments. 48 shares vs 23 comments. Shares drive reach, so the content is genuinely "send this to a mate" worthy.
  • Right people, right place. Reach is concentrated in Brighton, Worthing and Sussex, exactly your ICP geography.
  • Link taps up 20%. Small number but the only traffic signal moving the right way.

⚠️ Needs attention

  • Reach down 53%, profile visits down 41%. The two biggest month-on-month drops. Likely fewer or weaker-hooked posts in the back half, or last month had a spike to beat.
  • Ads are propping up the numbers. 55.9% of views are paid. Organic reach is the soft spot to rebuild.
  • High follower churn. 178 follows but 129 unfollows. You are keeping less than 1 in 3 new follows.
  • Static posts barely register. Posts are 8.8% of interactions and 9.1% of views. Carousels and photos are not pulling.
  • Conversation is thin. 23 comments across all reels. Engagement is passive (watch and like) rather than reply.
  • Stories stay in-house. Big share of views but interactions are 81% existing followers, so Stories are not converting new viewers.

The recommendation

Post in the late-morning to early-afternoon window, roughly 11am to 1pm. That gives the reel a few hours to gather early views, likes and shares before the 3pm to 6pm activity peak, which is when the algorithm decides whether to push it wider. Avoid posting after 9pm or overnight. Note: this curve is the Monday view, so pull Tuesday to Sunday next month for a full seven-day heatmap.

The churn is the real story here

You earned 178 follows but lost 129, so only 49 stuck. That much churn usually means new followers arrive from a strong reel, then don't see a clear, consistent reason to stay. The fix is a tight content rhythm so people know what they signed up for. More on that below.

08

What to do next 30 days

1

Go reel-heavy with the cyan-keyword hook

Aim for 4 to 5 reels a week in your education and behind-the-scenes formats. Keep the white-text plus one-cyan-keyword cover. It is your proven winner. Cut or repurpose static posts into reels.

2

Stop the follower leak

Set 3 content pillars so a new follower instantly knows what they will get (e.g. shoot BTS, founder lessons, client results). Pin your three best reels so your profile sells the account in 5 seconds.

3

Turn views into conversation and DMs

End every reel with a question or a "comment a word for the script" call to action. Put a ManyChat comment-trigger on your best performers so that reach becomes booked calls, not just likes.

4

Post into the 11am to 1pm window

Give each reel runway before the 3 to 6pm activity peak. Build the full seven-day active-times picture next month to sharpen this further.

5

Rebuild organic reach

More than half your views are paid right now. Lean into the "send to a mate" angles that already travel (drone, BTS, pricing, on-camera tips) to lift organic shares and reduce the reliance on ad spend.

6

Push the profile and the link harder

54 link taps off 29k reach is low. Tighten the bio call to action and use a link sticker on your best Stories so the traffic that does land actually converts.

7

Connect the API so this builds itself
